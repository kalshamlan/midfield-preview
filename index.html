01 — The Academy

Quality over quantity

Midfield Football Academy provides a professional, safe training environment that teaches children football and develops their technical, physical, and mental abilities through structured programs for every age stage. Our philosophy is simple: fewer players per group, more attention per player — every child is seen, corrected, and encouraged in every session.

We believe player development starts with building character. Values come first — then the fun, and then the skill.

Membership terms

  • Next month's fee is due on the 28th of each month, with a grace period until the end of day on the 30th.
  • If payment is not completed within the grace period, the player's place is released to the waiting list.
  • Fees are non-refundable and memberships cannot be frozen — absences are not credited or carried over.
  • While enrolled, players may not train with another football academy or club — school teams excepted.
  • Match and tournament selection is decided by the technical staff.
